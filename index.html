EDITORIAL PROFILE

The Chronicler's Creed

“Journalism without courageous skepticism is mere press release; literature without moral clarity is mere decoration. The duty of the reporter is to preserve public memory and speak truth when silence is most rewarded.”

Olukorede Yishau is an accomplished Nigerian journalist, editor, and author known for his significant contributions to both the media industry and contemporary African literature. He began his professional journalism career in 1999 at The Source magazine before joining the legendary investigative team at Tell magazine.

For nearly two decades, he has been a pillar at The Nation newspaper—one of Nigeria’s premier broadsheets—where he currently serves as Associate Editor and United States Bureau Chief based in New York.

His reporting portfolio spans the high-stakes intersections of politics, capital market regulations, aviation safety crises, and human interest narratives. Beyond news reporting, Yishau's regular opinion columns, notably Above Whispers, The Sunday Column, and Life in the Big Apple, have earned widespread acclaim for their trenchant clarity and ethical conviction.

FLAGSHIP INQUIRIES

Major Investigations & Special Projects

Deep forensic inquiries detailing reporting methods, findings, and societal impact.

CASE STUDY 01

The Pulpit and the Palace: Faith Merchants and Political Despots

Multi-Part Series • The Nation

01 The Core Investigation

Investigated how high-profile religious leaders were strategically co-opted by state actors to sanitize electoral malpractice, disarm public dissent, and legitimize human rights abuses in exchange for land grants and state protections.

02 Investigative Methods

Confidential whistleblower interviews, financial paper trails tracking tax exemptions and state allocations, audio recordings of closed-door partisan blessings, and extensive cross-state field inquiries.

03 Key Findings & Impact

Ignited a nationwide discourse on state-religion entanglement, cited in legislative debates on charity oversight, and inspired Yishau’s acclaimed novel In the Name of Our Father.

CASE STUDY 02

The Bleeding Floor: Uncovering Systemic Stock Manipulation

Financial Investigation • Winner: NMMA Capital Market Reporter (2013)

01 The Core Investigation

Probed orchestrated price inflation on the trading floor of the Nigerian Stock Exchange, where colluding brokers and corporate executives artificially bid up share values prior to dumping assets on unsuspecting pension savers.

02 Investigative Methods

Forensic examination of trade volume spikes, cross-checking registrar books, confidential interviews with disgruntled accounting staff, and comparative pricing models.

03 Key Findings & Impact

Triggered regulatory sanctions by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), leading to mandatory electronic tracking of share transactions and stricter compliance requirements.

CASE STUDY 03

Flying on a Wing and a Prayer: Air Safety Deficits in West Africa

Aviation Exposé • Winner: NMMA Aviation Industry Reporter (2003)

01 The Core Investigation

Undercover examination of airline maintenance corners cut by commercial carriers, pilot fatigue rosters exceeding civil aviation limits, and degraded instrument landing systems (ILS) across domestic airports.

02 Investigative Methods

Airside observations, leaked maintenance logbooks, interviews with certified aircraft engineers, and comparative benchmarking against International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) standards.

03 Key Findings & Impact

Compelled the Federal Ministry of Aviation to inaugurate an emergency audit committee, resulting in grounding of non-compliant aircraft and renewal of airport navigational aids.
