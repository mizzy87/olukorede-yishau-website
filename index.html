About Me

Olukorede Yishau is an acclaimed Nigerian journalist and author. With a career spanning several years at The Nation newspaper, he has established himself as a critical voice in African journalism.

His work explores the intersection of politics, society, and human rights. Beyond the newsroom, he is the author of critically acclaimed novels including "In the Name of Our Father" and "Vaults of Secrets".
