Executive Leadership

For more than a quarter of a century, Olukorede Yishau has operated at the nexus of high-stakes investigative journalism, literary storytelling, and transatlantic strategic communications. As United States Bureau Chief and Associate Editor for The Nation, he has reported from the corridors of Capitol Hill to the boardrooms of West Africa.

His published literary works—including the Nigeria Prize for Literature longlisted In the Name of Our Father, Vaults of Secrets, and After The End—demonstrate an unmatched mastery of human psychology, state power, and narrative tension. Through Yishau Strategic Media & Advisory, he brings this forensic rigor and editorial authority directly to institutional leaders:
