Methodology & Rigor

My Process

A battle-tested four-stage architecture developed over 25+ years in national newsrooms and literary presses.

01

Discovery & Forensic Research

Every memorable piece starts with immersive inquest. I comb through public records, conducted field interviews, cross-examine whistleblower testimonies, and map out the underlying human stakes.

Outcome: Comprehensive Dossier & Verified Facts
02

Narrative Architecture

Facts alone do not persuade; architecture does. I design the narrative trajectory, pacing, character dilemmas, and rhetorical arcs to ensure maximum emotional resonance and cognitive retention.

Outcome: Structural Blueprint & Thematic Cadence
03

Refinement & Editorial Polish

Relentless sentence-level craftsmanship. Prose is stripped of superfluous clutter, rhythm is calibrated, and legal/ethical invariants are audited to ensure bulletproof authority.

Outcome: Pristine Literary & Broadsheet Prose
04

Launch, Syndication & Dialogue

Publication is not the finish line; it is the ignition point. I coordinate syndication across national dailies, diplomatic bureaus, book clubs, and academic forums to spark enduring civic impact.

Outcome: Lasting Cultural & Institutional Impact
Biographical Narrative

About Me

For more than a quarter of a century, I have dedicated my life to the uncompromising pursuit of truth and the craft of storytelling. As an investigative journalist and United States Bureau Chief for The Nation, I have reported from the trenches of military decrees to the halls of Capitol Hill in Washington D.C.

My novels, including the Nigeria Prize for Literature longlisted In the Name of Our Father, Vaults of Secrets, and After The End, explore the intimate human cost of religious hypocrisy, political tyranny, and social dogma. Whether writing a front-page broadsheet exposé or a 300-page novel, my guiding conviction remains unchanged:

"A story that doesn't challenge power or comfort the afflicted is merely decorative. I build digital and narrative experiences that leave an indelible mark on the conscience."
