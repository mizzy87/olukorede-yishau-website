## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OPENING BIOGRAPHY

**Olukorede Yishau** is an acclaimed Nigerian investigative journalist, editor, novelist, and columnist with over two and a half decades of continuous practice at the forefront of African print and digital journalism. Currently serving as **Associate Editor** and **United States Bureau Chief** for *The Nation*—one of Nigeria’s most influential national daily newspapers—Yishau has documented the socio-political transformations of modern Nigeria and its diaspora through penetrating investigations, high-impact public interest reporting, and thought leadership.

A quadruple laureate of the prestigious **Nigeria Media Merit Award (NMMA)**—Nigeria's highest peer-reviewed journalism honour—Yishau is renowned for his versatility across diverse beats, including capital markets, the aviation sector, political corruption, governance, and human-interest features. His weekly column, which has featured under titles such as *Above Whispers* and *The Sunday Column*, alongside his trans-Atlantic dispatches in *Life in the Big Apple*, have consistently engaged policymakers, civil society, and international observers.

Beyond newspapers, Yishau is an established figure in contemporary African literature. His debut novel, *In the Name of Our Father* (2018), was longlisted for the prestigious **Nigeria Prize for Literature (NLNG 2021)** and is widely adopted in university curricula across Nigeria as a primary text on journalism ethics, state tyranny, and religious hypocrisy. His subsequent books—*Vaults of Secrets* (2020), *After the End* (2024), and *Black Market* (2026)—affirm his unique stature as a writer capable of bridging rigorous investigative reporting with luminous narrative craftsmanship.

## 2. PROFESSIONAL PROFILE & EDITORIAL PHILOSOPHY

### Editorial Mission
To practice journalism as a vehicle of civic accountability, ethical scrutiny, and democratic renewal. Whether dissecting high-finance regulatory lapses on the Nigerian Stock Exchange, exposing aviation safety failures, or chronicling the lived struggles of African immigrant communities in the West, Yishau’s work is characterized by thorough documentation, rigorous fact-verification, human empathy, and stylistic clarity.

### Areas of Specialization
- **Investigative & Accountability Reporting:** Unearthing institutional malfeasance, political patronage, human rights violations, and financial mismanagement.
- **Economic & Capital Markets Journalism:** Dissecting market trends, securities regulations, commercial banking stability, and macro-fiscal policy.
- **Aviation & Infrastructure Analysis:** Investigating aviation safety protocols, regulatory oversight by civil aviation authorities, and transport infrastructure deficits.
- **Narrative Non-Fiction & Longform Writing:** Crafting compelling, character-driven deep dives that humanize systemic socio-political crises.
- **Diaspora & Transnational Affairs:** Chronicling the migration pathways, cultural identity renegotiations, and economic contributions of the African diaspora.
- **Editorial Newsroom Leadership:** Directing foreign bureaus, mentoring early-career reporters, enforcing editorial ethics, and guiding publication strategy.

## 4. SELECTED PUBLISHED JOURNALISM BY BEAT

### 4.1 Investigative Journalism & Governance Exposés

#### 1. *"Echoes of Despotism: Inside the Dark Machinations of Dictatorship and the Press"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation*
- **Focus:** A historical-investigative reconstruction of state-sponsored intimidation, secret service surveillance against journalists, and the resilient underground networks of the press.
- **Significance:** Illustrated how the suppression of truth under military regimes sowed the seeds of contemporary democratic dysfunctions. Led to widespread debates on press freedom guarantees in Nigeria's National Assembly.

#### 2. *"The Anatomy of a Regulatory Capture: How Financial Watchdogs Slept as Billions Vanished"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation* / Business & Economy Special Report
- **Focus:** Multi-part investigation into insider trading, non-performing insider loans, and lax enforcement by banking regulatory institutions.
- **Significance:** Awarded **NMMA Capital Market Reporter of the Year (2013)**. Cited by market analysts and legislative committees reviewing investment and securities acts.

#### 3. *"Subsidies of Illusion: The Phantom Vessels and the Sovereign Fuel Scam"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation*
- **Focus:** Tracking vessel manifests, offshore transshipment claims, and petroleum subsidy payment claims. Revealed how non-existent product deliveries siphoned billions from the national treasury.
- **Impact:** Provided foundational media pressure that precipitated nationwide parliamentary investigations and anti-corruption prosecutions.

---

### 4.2 Politics & Public Affairs

#### 1. *"The Godfather Phenomenon: The Politics of Rent-Seeking and Democratic Capture"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation* (Sunday Column)
- **Focus:** Deconstructing the patron-client structures in state politics, where unelected political powerbrokers dictate state treasury disbursements and party candidate selections.
- **Significance:** Highly syndicated across national syndication portals; recognized with the **NMMA Columnist of the Year (2015)**.

#### 2. *"Transition on a Knife-Edge: The 2023 Electoral Calculus and the Diaspora Vote"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation* (US Bureau Dispatches)
- **Focus:** Comprehensive analysis of voter sentiment, the logistical hurdles of diaspora enfranchisement, and international diplomatic posturing ahead of Nigeria's pivotal 2023 elections.

#### 3. *"The Cost of Loyalty: Cabinets, Cronyism, and the Erosion of Technocracy"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation*
- **Focus:** Critical assessment of ministerial appointments, evaluating whether ministerial rosters were crafted to reward political campaign machinery rather than solve existential macroeconomic crises.

---

### 4.3 Capital Markets, Banking & Economic Governance

#### 1. *"Market Volatility, Penny Stocks, and the Plight of the Retail Investor"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation*
- **Focus:** An analytical exposé on predatory market manipulation on the Nigerian Stock Exchange (now NGX), showing how ordinary pension savers were misled by hyped share placements.
- **Significance:** Catalyzed regulatory revisions regarding initial public offer disclosures and retail investor education guidelines.

#### 2. *"Monetary Tightening vs. Real-Sector Survival: The Dilemma of Central Banking"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation* (Financial Analysis)
- **Focus:** An examination of hawkish interest rate spikes, inflation trends, and the corresponding strangulation of small-and-medium enterprise lending.

---

### 4.4 Aviation Industry & Infrastructure Investigations

#### 1. *"Danger in the Skies: Maintenance Lapses, Aging Fleets, and Civil Aviation Oversight"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation* / *The Source*
- **Focus:** Undercover investigation into runway navigation safety deficits, pilot fatigue protocols, and aircraft maintenance corners cut by budget carriers.
- **Significance:** Won the **NMMA Aviation Industry Reporter of the Year (2003)**. Prompted emergency regulatory audits by the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA).

#### 2. *"The Grounded Ambitions: The Never-Ending Tale of a National Carrier"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation*
- **Focus:** Tracing the decades of stalled policy, billions expended on branding exercises, and the geopolitical horse-trading behind the proposed national flag carrier project.

---

### 4.5 Features, Human Interest & Diaspora Chronicles (*Life in the Big Apple*)

#### 1. *"Subway Dreams and Winter Blues: The Lived Realities of the New African Migrant in New York"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation* (Life in the Big Apple Series)
- **Focus:** Lyrical narrative feature exploring undocumented immigrant struggles, credential non-recognition, the cold solitude of the American metropolis, and resilient remittance economies.

#### 2. *"Between Two Worlds: Second-Generation Identity and the Search for Ancestral Belonging"*
- **Publication:** *The Nation*
- **Focus:** Cultural study of young African-Americans and African-British individuals navigating heritage, linguistic alienation, and the modern "Return to Africa" cultural pilgrimage.
