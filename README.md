# Journalism Portfolio & Professional Dossier — Olukorede Yishau

Official digital journalism portfolio and archival professional dossier for **Olukorede Yishau** — Associate Editor & United States Bureau Chief at *The Nation* newspaper, 4-time Nigeria Media Merit Award (NMMA) laureate, and celebrated author of *In the Name of Our Father*, *Vaults of Secrets*, *After the End*, and *Black Market*.

---

## 🌟 Live Demo & Deployment

This project is a modern, zero-dependency client web application built with semantic HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. It can be deployed directly with **GitHub Pages** in under 60 seconds with zero build steps.

### Enabling GitHub Pages:
1. Go to your repository **Settings** on GitHub.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment** > **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/ (root)`.
4. Click **Save**. Your portfolio will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

## 🎨 Editorial Design System

- **Deep Navy Blue (`#0B1F33`):** Main masthead, header, cover section, and primary editorial headings.
- **Off-White (`#F7F5F0`):** Body background canvas for readability.
- **Charcoal (`#252525`):** Body typography.
- **Gold (`#C9A227`):** Accent lines, section numbering, milestone dates, and award highlights.
- **White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast cards and article containers.

## 📰 Portfolio Highlights

- **Hero & Profile:** Cover masthead with custom author portrait, credentials, and live career metrics counter.
- **Selected Works Archive:** Interactive filter tabs (*Investigative*, *Politics & Governance*, *Capital Markets & Economy*, *Aviation & Safety*, *Features & Diaspora*) with real-time keyword search.
- **Reporting Brief Modals:** In-depth breakdown of reporting methodology, confidential sourcing, and societal impact.
- **Flagship Investigations:** Case study deep dives into *The Pulpit and the Palace*, *The Bleeding Floor*, and *Air Safety Deficits*.
- **3D Literary Bookshelf:** Visual catalogue of published novels and short story collections.
- **Honours & Distinctions:** 4× NMMA awards timeline and NLNG Prize for Literature longlist citation.
- **1-Click PDF / Print Dossier:** Print-optimized stylesheet (`@media print`) that formats the portfolio cleanly into a multi-page PDF document.
